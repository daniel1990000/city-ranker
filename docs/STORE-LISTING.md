## Full description (1987/4000)
```
Step into the cage. Cage Kings is a hard-hitting 3D MMA fighting game built for phones, with realistic fighters, simple touch controls and real depth.

STRIKE, GRAPPLE, FINISH
• Tap to jab, swipe for hooks, uppercuts, body shots and kicks
• Hold to block, swipe to slip, and time it right to open a counter
• Shoot a takedown, or sprawl to stop one
• Fight for position on the ground and land ground-and-pound
• Win the submission battle by tapping faster than your opponent
• Finish by KO, TKO, submission, or go the distance to the judges

REAL FIGHTS, NOT BUTTON MASHING
Every move costs stamina. Fighters get rocked, hurt to the body and slowed by worn legs. Mash punches and a smart opponent will cover up and punish you. Five fighting styles to face: brawler, boxer, kicker, wrestler and submission specialist.

BUILD A CHAMPION
• Career mode: climb from the amateurs to a title belt
• Six weight classes, men's and women's
• 48 original fighters to collect, level up and train
• Six venues, from a back-street gym to a rooftop under the lights

COME BACK EVERY DAY
• Daily rewards and a free fighter pack every day
• A monthly Season Pass reward track
• Leaderboards on Google Play Games for wins, knockouts and best win

FAIR PLAY, CLEARLY SHOWN
• Every fighter can be earned for free by playing
• Pack odds are shown before you open or buy a pack
• A visible pity counter guarantees an epic fighter within a set number of packs
• Duplicate fighters turn into shards, so nothing is wasted
• Ads are optional rewards labelled "Watch ad". Occasional ad breaks never appear in your first session, and No Ads removes them
• Cloud save with Google Play Games

New to MMA? A short first-fight lesson teaches the controls, and you can skip it.

Cage Kings is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases, including random items (fighter packs). Paid packs are not available in some countries. Contains ads. Fighting violence, no blood. All fighters, names and venues are fictional.
```

## Honesty notes for the owner
- No online fights are promised: only Play Games leaderboards, matching the launch plan.
- "Includes random items" must also be ticked in Play Console (content rating and in-app products), to match the line at the end of the description.
- The pack and fairness section is true to the game today: odds on the pack screen, visible pity counter (guaranteed epic), duplicates to shards, every fighter earnable free, paid packs off in Belgium and other listed countries.
- "No blood" and "fictional fighters" support the 12+/Teen rating and keep clear of real people and brands.
- The free daily pack, daily rewards and the skippable first-fight lesson are all in 0.2.0/0.3.0 release notes.
- Words to avoid in any future edit: UFC, Octagon, EA, any real fighter or brand name, "PvP", "online battles", "multiplayer".
