Cage Kings privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026. Cage Kings is made by Daniel Spurr ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

What the game stores on your phone

Your progress (fighters, career, coins and gems, settings) is saved on your phone. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also saved to your Google account so you can restore it on another phone.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game receives a purchase token from Google Play to confirm what you bought.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK, the EU and some US states) you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the settings. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads; see Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Ads in Cage Kings are limited to content suitable for teens. Buying "No Ads" removes the ad breaks between fights.

Play statistics (optional)

If you agree, the game sends anonymous play statistics and error reports (for example which fights are too hard, and crashes) so we can improve it. They carry a random install id, never your name, email or contacts. You can turn this off at any time in the settings; it is off unless you agree.

Children

Cage Kings is rated for players aged 12 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete all data the game stores on your phone by uninstalling it or clearing its storage. To ask about or delete any data held by us, contact us at the support email shown on the game's Google Play page.
